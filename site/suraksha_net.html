Step 1 · Dataset audit

The public datasets overlap with themselves and each other

If the same footage ends up in both training and test sets, the reported accuracy measures memory rather than detection. Before training anything, we fingerprinted every clip and grouped clips that share a source.

11

exact duplicate files in RWF-2000. Six of them sit across its official train and validation split.

10

YouTube source videos that appear in both RWF-2000 and SFD, covering 57 RWF clips.

137

confirmed near-duplicate clip pairs (perceptual-hash distance ≤ 6), plus 677 borderline pairs.

1,122

source groups. Each group goes wholly into train, validation or test, never across them.

The 5 duplicate copies inside RWF-2000 were removed, leaving 2,645 clips. Every split file is locked by its SHA-256 hash in LOCK.json, so the test sets cannot change between experiments.

Step 3 · Models

Finding · Distillation

Learning from VideoMAE helps the small model most on unfamiliar footage

MotionLite was trained twice per split: once on labels alone, and once with VideoMAE's predictions as a second target. With two seeds each, the gain is clear on unseen AIRTLab (+0.067, far above the seed spread) and on RWF-2000 (+0.013). On the other splits the difference is within seed noise.

On unseen AIRTLab, distilled MotionLite (0.829) scores higher than fine-tuned VideoMAE (0.792), with 29× fewer parameters.

Finding · Leakage

On these datasets, leaky splits did not inflate scores in a consistent way

We compared a random split with a group-aware split of the same clips. Some models scored higher on the random split, others lower, and every difference sits inside the confidence intervals. The audit still matters: the overlap is real, and the clean splits are what make the unseen-dataset numbers trustworthy.

Limits

What these numbers do not show

  • The SFD and AIRTLab test sets are small (47 and 52 clips), so a few clips change the score noticeably.
  • MobileNetV3-TSM and the frozen probes have one run each, so they have no seed spread.
  • All clips are short, trimmed segments. A deployed system would run on continuous footage with far fewer violent moments.
  • Latency was measured on one laptop. Edge hardware will be slower.
